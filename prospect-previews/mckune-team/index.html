The business case

Make the beautiful part accountable to the business result.

Visual upgrades are hypotheses to test, not guaranteed conversion improvements. A simple attribution framework would make it possible to learn whether a design, neighborhood or CTA change creates a stronger seller pipeline.

01 · REACHNeighborhood mailer

Distinguish campaigns and audiences with a dedicated URL or QR source.

02 · INTERESTQR visit

See what portion of campaign response reaches the right seller experience.

03 · LEADHome value inquiry

Measure completed requests, not just scans or page views.

04 · RELATIONSHIPSeller conversation

Connect appropriate inquiries to held consultations and listing appointments.

05 · OUTCOMESigned listing

Compare eventual listing results by campaign while respecting CRM and client privacy.

A measured trial, not a redesign gamble. Start with one community-specific mailer, confirm current tracking and lead handling first, and test a stronger digital presentation without disrupting what already works. Any real-world property image, brokerage identifier, market statistic, consent language or advertising claim would require suitable rights and approval before use.
