A thoughtful starting point

Four questions. One useful conversation.

Before making recommendations, I would want to understand what's already working for the local office.

Does Savant already connect registration source to attendance?

If yes, that is a strong foundation. We would build on the existing answer, not replace it.

Which event outcomes matter most to the local advisory team?

Registration volume, quality of conversations, appointments held, and longer-term fit can point to very different next actions.

How much of the event process is controlled locally?

National branding, compliant follow-up, and approved systems may already be centrally managed.

Could one small improvement sharpen the next mailing?

A channel tag, simplified response path, or clearer post-event reporting may be more useful than a larger campaign rebuild.
