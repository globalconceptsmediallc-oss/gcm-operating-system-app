01 · What caught our attention

The postcard already proves you believe in marketing.

The opportunity is not “do more marketing.” It is making the investment you already make easier to measure, follow up, and extend across channels.

The current mail piece

Strong local branding, multiple service lines, a direct call/text CTA and a QR code. The main opportunity is turning that attention into a trackable campaign path.

Clear campaign offer

Give the property owner one primary reason to respond instead of asking the piece to carry every service message at once.

Dedicated destination

Send QR and digital traffic to a campaign-specific seller page rather than leaving attribution buried inside general website traffic.

Follow-up beyond mail

Use the same offer in paid social, search and retargeting so the postcard becomes the first touch — not the last.

The idea: keep the recognizable Curri brand, but connect the mail piece to a measurable seller-acquisition system.

05 · Follow-up beyond the mailbox

One campaign can keep working after the postcard is thrown away.

Prospecting creative

Reach the same homeowner/property-owner profile digitally with the same message.

  • Meta seller-awareness campaign
  • Google Search for high-intent seller queries
  • Dedicated source/UTM tracking by channel

Retargeting

Keep John visible to people who showed interest but were not ready to respond immediately.

  • “Still considering selling?” creative
  • Short market videos and recent-sale proof
  • Seller education and consultation CTA

Lead response

Turn the inquiry into a real conversation quickly.

  • Immediate email/text confirmation
  • Internal lead alert with campaign source
  • Follow-up sequence when a lead does not book

Measurement

Know what the campaign actually creates.

  • QR scans and landing-page visits
  • Calls, forms and booked consultations
  • Source-to-lead and source-to-close where available
