THE FIRST 90 DAYS

Three focused phases.

Each phase builds on the one before it so we can make decisions from real information instead of assumptions.

01
DAYS 1–30

Build the Foundation

Finish the new digital foundation, make the business measurable, and make sure the website accurately represents the operation.

  • Finalize and launch the new Agnor Aviation website
  • Move the web infrastructure away from the current hosting setup
  • Establish GitHub and Cloudflare as the performance/security foundation
  • Correct existing technical and SEO issues
  • Set up GA4, Google Search Console, and conversion measurement
  • Review and strengthen Google Business Profile
  • Organize maintenance, repair, operational support, and hangar paths around real customer intent
OutcomeA fast, professional, measurable website that accurately represents Agnor Aviation and gives us a clean platform to build from.
02
DAYS 31–60

Build Visibility Around the Right Work

Start building search visibility around the specific work Agnor wants—not simply around a generic “aircraft maintenance Melbourne” search.

  • Research how aircraft owners, fleet operators, and organizations search for Agnor's services
  • Build the first service-specific landing pages around high-value capabilities
  • Evaluate geographic opportunities beyond Melbourne where Agnor can realistically serve customers
  • Strengthen aircraft, repair, inspection, and special-mission content
  • Improve local and regional search visibility
  • Monitor site health and search performance
  • Begin building content around the maintenance and repair work worth winning
OutcomeAgnor begins being positioned around the work it actually wants to win, in the markets where the right customer may be searching.
03
DAYS 61–90

Turn the Website Into a Growth System

Use the first two months of data to decide where more time, attention, and marketing investment can create the greatest return.

  • Review search visibility, inquiries, and conversion data
  • Identify which services and markets are showing the strongest opportunity
  • Improve the pages most likely to create high-value conversations
  • Evaluate paid search only where the economics justify it
  • Establish an ongoing marketing and reporting rhythm
  • Review media, advertising, and partnership opportunities before money is committed
  • Choose the first operational website improvement—forms, document access, maintenance resources, or hangar-capacity tools
OutcomeAt 90 days, we know what is generating attention, what deserves more investment, and what the next phase should be.
