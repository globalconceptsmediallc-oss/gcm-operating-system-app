The strategic distinction

The Bassett creative invites deliberation and customization; the Stressless creative encourages a time-bound comfort and value decision. GCM would measure them separately while reporting both against meaningful showroom and sold-order outcomes.

02 / Work with what is already there

A practical starting point—not a rebuild.

Indian River Furniture describes a 32,000-square-foot showroom, hand-tested furniture and design assistance. A publicly visible contact form has asked visitors “How did you hear about us?” That suggests a useful attribution conversation already exists; whether it carries through to final purchases is something to verify.

01

Product-specific response

Assign each mailer a clean campaign identifier, QR destination or dedicated promotion path where feasible. Do not rely on manufacturer-owned QR experiences alone to infer in-store results.

02

Showroom conversation

Make it simple for designers or salespeople to log whether a visit relates to Bassett, Stressless or another campaign, while recording the customer’s actual needs and stage.

03

Completed order

Connect consultations, written quotes and purchases to the appropriate campaign when customers opt in and available systems support it. Use aggregate reporting for executive decisions.

03 / Proposed measurement path

Measure beyond delivery and response.

This six-step chain is a recommendation, not a statement about the software or analytics Indian River Furniture currently uses.

Customer privacy, consent, manufacturer requirements and existing point-of-sale processes determine which joins are practical. Where exact attribution is not possible, GCM would report the limits rather than manufacture certainty.

An example of the decision this enables:

If the Stressless offer generates more quick showroom visits while Bassett produces fewer but larger custom orders, success should be assessed against sales value and cost per acquired order—not response counts alone. This is a hypothetical decision example, not a finding about existing campaign results.

06 / Questions worth discussing

Four questions for a 15-minute conversation.

When a customer walks in with a Bassett or Stressless promotion, does the sales system preserve which campaign brought them in?
How are custom-furniture quotes, deposits and final orders linked back to an original inquiry?
Are these manufacturer-coordinated campaigns, store-funded mailers or some combination—and what reporting is already available?
Would one monthly report comparing campaign cost, showroom traffic and sold-order value make the next advertising decision easier?
