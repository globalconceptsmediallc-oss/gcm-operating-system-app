01 · What caught our attention

The mail piece already has a strong direct-response job.

It gives the market a specific place, a specific week, a free evaluation, and a broad list of items Paramount is prepared to buy. That is a much stronger starting point than a generic “learn more” campaign. The opportunity is not to replace the flyer. It is to make the response path around it easier to find, track, and compare from one roadshow market to the next.

02 · The business question

The useful metric is not traffic. It is completed buying activity by market.

A rotating roadshow model creates a powerful measurement opportunity because every market is effectively its own campaign. If the source survives from the original mail piece through the event outcome, Paramount can learn which markets, offers, response paths, and media combinations deserve more investment.

04 · Where measurement can break

The biggest leak is usually between the campaign and the in-person transaction.

Before the event

  • The mail piece sends everyone to a general website instead of a Viera-specific destination
  • QR, phone and web responses are not separated by market or mail drop
  • Local search demand for “gold buyer,” “coin buyer,” or related needs is not connected to the event
  • Someone interested in attending cannot quickly confirm dates, venue, hours and what Paramount buys

At the event and after

  • Walk-ins are counted but their original campaign source is unknown
  • Calls and appointments are visible but do not connect to completed evaluations
  • Transactions are known operationally but cannot be compared back to the media source
  • The next roadshow starts without a clear market-by-market acquisition baseline
The goal is not a complicated reporting stack. It is one simple thread: Which campaign created which visit, evaluation, and transaction?

06 · What we would verify first

Before adding more media, verify what the current Viera campaign can already tell you.

Attribution

  • Can each mail drop or market use a unique URL, QR code, phone source or campaign ID?
  • Can calls, forms and site visits be tied specifically to the Viera roadshow?
  • Can event visits and evaluations retain that original campaign source?
  • Can completed purchases be summarized by market and source?

Local demand capture

  • Does a Viera roadshow page exist early enough to support local search and paid media?
  • Can the page rank or advertise around the actual buying categories and event location?
  • Are map, phone, QR and directions actions measurable separately?
  • Can retargeting or follow-up support people who show interest but do not attend immediately?
First principle: Measure the current roadshow path before recommending additional media spend.
