Private direct-mail growth review

Prepared for Window World of Space Coast

The postcard already gives homeowners a reason to respond. The opportunity is keeping that source measurable all the way to an installed job.

I received your fall direct-mail piece in Melbourne and reviewed the path from mailbox to website. The mailer is strong: a clear impact-window price point, financing, hurricane protection, My Safe Florida Home positioning, a project QR code, and a dedicated quote phone number.

Working premise: I would not start by changing the offer. I would start by making sure every response path—phone, QR, website form, estimate, and sold project—stays connected to the exact mail campaign that created it.

01 · What the mailer is already doing well

The offer is specific enough to create action.

Concrete price anchor

The piece leads with hurricane-impact windows starting at $947 installed and compares that against $1,500, giving the homeowner an immediate reason to evaluate the offer.

Multiple trust signals

Lifetime limited warranty, Made in USA, local ownership, hurricane-impact positioning, and My Safe Florida Home eligibility reduce uncertainty before the first call.

Direct response built in

The mailer includes a dedicated free-quote phone number plus a QR code tied to completed projects, giving the campaign more than one response path.

02 · What caught my attention

The tracking appears to begin at the mailer. The opportunity is preserving it after the first response.

The direct-mail phone number on the piece is different from the primary phone shown on the local website, which suggests some source tracking may already be in place. That is a good start. The next question is whether the same campaign identity survives when someone scans the QR code, visits later through search, submits the website form, schedules an estimate, and eventually buys.

The measurement gap worth testing: how many mailed households become website visits, calls, booked estimates, completed appointments, sold projects, and installed revenue—and which offer, geography, or creative produced them?

03 · The current campaign message

The postcard and website already have strong message continuity.

Impact-window offerThe mailer and current site both feature impact windows starting at $947, so the homeowner does not hit a completely different message after leaving the postcard.
FinancingThe postcard promotes 0% interest for 15 months, while the website also has a dedicated financing path.
Hurricane protectionThe direct-mail message is highly relevant to coastal Florida, and the local site has a dedicated impact-resistant window experience.
My Safe Florida HomeThe mailer gives the program prominent space. That can be used as a measurable campaign theme rather than only a badge on the creative.

04 · The attribution path

One postcard should remain identifiable from mailbox to revenue.

01DeliveryCampaign, mail date, ZIP/route, creative, offer, and household segment.
02ResponseTracked call, QR scan, campaign page, website form, or branded search.
03EstimateBooked, completed, canceled, rescheduled, product interest, and quote value.
04OutcomeSold job, installed revenue, financing used, product mix, and campaign ROI.

05 · A practical 90-day test

Instrument one real mail campaign before changing the media mix.

DAYS 1–30

Connect the Mailer

  • Campaign-specific landing path
  • Preserve the existing tracked phone
  • QR source capture
  • Form source capture
  • Estimate-stage attribution
  • Baseline response + close rates
DAYS 31–60

Measure the Sales Journey

  • Response by ZIP / route
  • Call vs. QR vs. form
  • Estimate show rate
  • Quote-to-sale conversion
  • Impact vs. non-impact interest
  • My Safe Florida Home influence
DAYS 61–90

Use the Evidence

  • Revenue by mail campaign
  • Cost per booked estimate
  • Cost per sold project
  • Best-performing geography
  • Best-performing offer
  • Next mail / digital decision
The goal: keep a strong direct-mail program, but make the next drop smarter because the prior one tells you exactly what produced appointments and installed revenue.

06 · Recommended first move

Do not rebuild the site. Build a campaign-specific response path.

The current local site already has the product, financing, trust, installation, and estimate infrastructure. The cleanest first test is to create a dedicated destination for one mail campaign that carries the same $947 impact-window message, the financing language, local project proof, and the My Safe Florida Home angle—then preserve the source all the way through the estimate and sale.

What I would keep

The current offer, local brand, free in-home estimate, impact-window emphasis, financing, project proof, and existing response channels.

What I would add

Campaign identity that survives beyond the first click or phone call so the next direct-mail decision is based on booked estimates and sold work rather than response alone.
