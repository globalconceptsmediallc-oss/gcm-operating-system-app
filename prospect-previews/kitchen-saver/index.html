WHAT I WOULD NOT CHANGE

Keep the strengths of the existing Kitchen Saver offer.

The public Kitchen Saver site already reinforces the core positioning behind the mailer: cabinet renewal/refacing, faster completion than a major renovation, custom upgrades, and consultation-based selling. The job is to preserve that message while improving local continuity and measurement.

01 · CREATIVE

Keep before-and-after front and center.

The transformation is easy to understand visually. The landing experience should continue that story immediately instead of forcing a mail recipient to interpret a generic page.

02 · SPEED

Keep the low-disruption promise prominent.

“As little as 3 days” is a strong differentiator because it answers one of the biggest homeowner objections to kitchen renovation: disruption.

03 · OFFER

Keep offer terms synchronized.

The mail piece, landing page, call handling, and follow-up should all reference the same active promotion and required financing disclosures. I would not independently change promotional or credit language.

04 · SALES

Measure the consultation, not just the click.

For this type of business, a successful campaign is not a website session. It is a qualified homeowner who schedules, attends, receives an estimate, and becomes a profitable project.
