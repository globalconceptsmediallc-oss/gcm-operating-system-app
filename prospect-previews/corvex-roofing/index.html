WHAT THE MAGAZINE PLACEMENT DOES WELL

It blends trust, education, and urgency.

  • Local editorial context
  • Free roof-readiness assessment

Give 321 Living its own source identity.

The QR code, landing page, phone path, and form should all carry a campaign/source value such as 321 Living + issue/date + creative so later reporting can distinguish magazine-driven leads from search, referral, or repeat business.

Measure the whole roofing funnel.

The real campaign result is not a scan. It is assessment scheduled → inspection completed → estimate issued → project won → revenue → future maintenance or referral.

Retarget the “not yet” homeowner.

Many roofing prospects are not ready to replace a roof the first day they see the ad. A clean campaign path creates a way to continue educating high-intent homeowners without losing the original source.

Use the profile story beyond print.

The leadership, local team, transparency, and “same local team, new ownership” story can support local landing pages, social proof, email follow-up, and sales materials rather than living only inside one magazine issue.

The timing is especially good because Corvex is building a newer brand on top of an established local operating history.

That makes attribution more important, not less. As print, search, reviews, referrals, and the legacy G&G recognition overlap, the reporting should show which sources introduce the new Corvex name and which ones actually create profitable roofing work.
