THE OPPORTUNITY I SEE

Keep the ad. Add source continuity from magazine to meeting.

Leonard Financial Group already has a polished website, clear fiduciary positioning, service segmentation, and a no-obligation introductory meeting path. The missing layer is campaign-specific attribution for local media placements.

WHAT THE AD ALREADY DOES WELL

It communicates the firm's positioning in seconds.

The ad leads with planning, then organizes the practice around financial planning, wealth management, and wealth protection. It also puts Jeffrey Leonard, CFP®, directly in front of the reader, which gives the placement a personal local face.

  • Clear fiduciary / planning-led positioning
  • Named local principal
  • Three understandable service categories
  • Direct phone and web response
  • Complimentary-review CTA
Give 321 Living a dedicated campaign destination.

A short branded URL or QR destination can mirror the ad's message, preserve required disclosures, and automatically identify the issue/source when the prospect reaches the firm.

Track print-driven calls separately.

A campaign-specific call path makes it possible to compare phone response from 321 Living against organic search, referral, parish/community exposure, and other local media.

Preserve source after the first inquiry.

The original campaign should stay attached through introductory meeting scheduled, meeting completed, planning engagement, and later permitted CRM stages rather than disappearing after the first contact.

Use the data to improve the next issue.

Once source continuity exists, Leonard Financial Group can compare issue, creative, offer, audience, response type, and meeting conversion before deciding what local print deserves more investment.

This is not a website-rebuild pitch.

The current site already does a lot of the heavy lifting: fiduciary explanation, planning philosophy, service education, team credibility, contact forms, and meeting scheduling. The opportunity is to connect local print exposure to that infrastructure without losing the source along the way.

THE ATTRIBUTION PATH

One reader should remain measurable from magazine exposure to business outcome.

The campaign source should survive each handoff.

01

Exposure

321 Living issue, community placement, referral, search, social, or other source.

02

Response

Campaign URL, QR scan, phone call, contact form, or branded search.

03

Meeting

Introductory meeting requested, scheduled, completed, canceled, or rescheduled.

04

Engagement

Qualified planning relationship or other permitted next stage.

05

Outcome

Permitted CRM result tied back to the original source history.

A PRACTICAL 90-DAY TEST

Instrument one local print campaign before changing the media mix.

The fastest way to prove value is to connect one real placement end to end and see what the current reporting misses.

DAYS 1–30

Connect the Placement

Create a campaign-specific path and establish the baseline.

  • 321 Living campaign ID
  • Dedicated landing URL / QR
  • Tracked phone response
  • Form source capture
  • Meeting-source continuity
  • Baseline dashboard
DAYS 31–60

Preserve the Journey

Keep the original source visible after the prospect moves into the firm's normal process.

  • Inquiry-to-meeting tracking
  • Meeting status
  • First-touch + assisted source
  • Follow-up measurement
  • Campaign-to-engagement reporting
  • Compliance-approved page variants
DAYS 61–90

Use the Evidence

Evaluate whether local print is creating meaningful downstream movement.

  • Response by source
  • Meeting conversion
  • Show rate
  • Qualified opportunity rate
  • Cost per qualified response
  • Next local-media decision
The goal: make a good local-media placement accountable to the relationship it creates—not just the website visit it generates.

WHAT I WOULD KEEP

The firm's strongest positioning is already clear.

The public website reinforces the same themes as the print ad: fiduciary planning, personalized financial roadmaps, wealth management, wealth protection, and a strong focus on individual needs and goals.

01 · FIDUCIARY

Keep planning ahead of products.

The firm's “planning, not products” positioning is distinctive and should stay consistent from print to campaign page to introductory meeting.

02 · PERSONAL

Keep Jeff visible.

The ad works because the reader sees a real local professional, not an anonymous financial-services brand. That personal continuity should carry into the digital path.

03 · SIMPLE

Keep the three-service structure.

Financial planning, wealth management, and wealth protection are easy to understand and already align with the current site.

04 · MEASUREMENT

Add campaign identity beneath it.

The improvement is operational: make it possible to tell whether 321 Living created the inquiry and whether that inquiry ultimately became a qualified relationship.

Financial-services compliance stays in control.

Any public-facing campaign page, investment language, tax-related language, insurance statement, performance claim, or disclosure change should remain subject to Leonard Financial Group's existing supervisory and compliance process. This concept is about attribution architecture and campaign measurement, not independent financial advice or claims.
