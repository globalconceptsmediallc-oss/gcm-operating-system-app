THE OPERATING RULES

Measurement underneath the approved marketing—not around it.

The attribution layer should preserve Wadadli's approved messaging and required disclosures while improving the connection between campaign source and downstream business movement.

01 · MESSAGE

Preserve approved language.

Campaign pages and follow-up should carry forward the firm's approved educational positioning, event details, disclosures, and calls to action rather than inventing new financial claims.

02 · DISCLOSURE

Keep compliance attached.

Required investment-adviser, insurance, risk, radio-placement, and other disclosures should remain visible wherever the campaign requires them.

03 · ATTRIBUTION

Track behavior, not private advice.

The reporting need is campaign performance: source, RSVP, attendance, appointment, and permitted CRM outcomes—not the substance of an individual's private financial circumstances.

04 · DECISIONS

Let the evidence guide the next spend.

The output should make it easier to decide which media, lists, events, and response paths deserve more investment before the next campaign launches.

Financial-services compliance stays in control.

Any public-facing campaign page, ad copy, seminar language, tax-related statement, investment language, or disclosure change should remain subject to Wadadli Financial Group's existing supervisory and compliance approval process. This concept is about attribution architecture and campaign measurement, not independent financial or tax claims.
