THE OPERATING RULES

Measurement without creating compliance problems.

The measurement layer should sit underneath the approved marketing—not independently rewrite financial, investment, tax, insurance, or legislative claims.

01 · MESSAGE

Preserve approved language.

Campaign pages and follow-up should carry forward the firm's approved positioning, event descriptions, disclosures, and calls to action rather than inventing new claims.

02 · DISCLOSURE

Keep disclosures attached.

Required broker-dealer, investment-adviser, insurance, risk, and other compliance language should remain visible wherever the campaign requires it.

03 · ATTRIBUTION

Track behavior, not sensitive advice.

The reporting need is campaign performance: source, RSVP, attendance, appointment, and permitted CRM outcomes—not the substance of a person's private financial circumstances.

04 · DECISIONS

Let evidence guide the next spend.

The useful output is a clearer view of which media, lists, event dates, and response paths deserve more investment before the next campaign launches.

Financial-services compliance stays in control.

Any public-facing campaign page, ad copy, seminar language, tax-related statement, investment language, or disclosure change should remain subject to McPherson Financial Group's existing supervisory and compliance approval process. This concept is about attribution architecture and campaign measurement, not independent financial or tax claims.
